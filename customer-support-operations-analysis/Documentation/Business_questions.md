# Business Questions

## Operations Performance → Operations Manager, Contact Centre Manager

1. Which support channels have the longest resolution times?
2. What percentage of tickets exceed SLA targets (`sla_breached`), and which channels account for the most breaches?
3. Which priority level experiences the highest escalation rate?
4. How do first response times (`first_response_time_hours`) and resolution times differ across support channels?
5. Which customer segments and subscription types experience the longest average resolution times?

---

## Customer & Product Analysis → Product Manager, Customer Experience Manager

6. Which products generate the highest number of support tickets?
7. Which subscription types have the highest SLA breach rates?
8. Which ticket categories occur most frequently across different products?
9. Which customer segments contact support most frequently, and what are their preferred contact channels?
10. Which geographic regions generate the highest ticket volume?

---

## Financial Impact → Finance Manager, Operations Director

11. Which payment methods are associated with the highest volume of payment- and billing-related support tickets?
12. Which subscription types account for the highest proportion of complex, high-resolution-time tickets?

---

## Quality & Customer Experience → Quality Assurance, Customer Success

13. Which ticket categories yield the lowest average customer satisfaction scores (`customer_satisfaction_score`)?
14. Which combination of product, category, and support channel creates the highest overall support workload?

---

## Strategic Improvement → Senior Leadership and Business Analysts

15. What trends over time (by creation date, complexity score, or escalation status) indicate key opportunities for operational process improvement?
